Everything you need

From weekly score entry to semester report cards — the entire workflow in one place.

Score Entry

Fast weekly entry for spelling, daily assignments, observations, and tests. Paste from Excel, auto-fill totals, live averages.

Auto Grading

A* through U computed automatically with the SPR formula — Written counts 20%, every other part 10%. Grades update instantly when a score changes.

Comparative Analysis

Cross-semester progress with movers, RAG indicators, SWOT analysis, and auto-generated teacher remarks.

Report Cards

Professional student report cards with SWOT, rankings, and parent-ready formatting. Print to PDF or batch-export to Excel.

Excel Import / Export

Import existing SPR workbooks with one click. Export back to the same format. Your data stays in this browser until you choose to export it or use encrypted Share & sync.

Privacy by Design

All data lives in your browser, stored encrypted. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on optional cloud sync or sharing — and then only an end-to-end encrypted copy leaves this device; the decryption key never does. No tracking, no analytics.

Privacy & security

The browser is the database: student data is stored encrypted on your device, and nothing is uploaded unless you turn on encrypted sync or sharing.

  • All data stored encrypted in your browser — nothing is uploaded by default
  • no server accounts, no analytics, no tracking — optional sync/share uploads only end-to-end encrypted bytes
  • Offline-ready: after your first successful visit, the app reopens without an internet connection
  • One-click backup file to move data between devices
  • One-click erase removes all data permanently

No accounts. No tracking. No analytics.
