Everything you need

From weekly score entry to semester report cards — the entire workflow in one place.

Score Entry

Fast weekly entry for spelling, daily assignments, observations, and tests. Paste from Excel, auto-fill totals, live averages.

Auto Grading

A* through U computed from your school’s exact weighted formula. Every grade updates instantly when a score changes.

Comparative Analysis

Cross-semester progress with movers, RAG indicators, SWOT analysis, and auto-generated teacher remarks.

Report Cards

Professional student report cards with SWOT, rankings, and parent-ready formatting. Print to PDF or batch-export to Excel.
