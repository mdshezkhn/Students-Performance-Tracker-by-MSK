About SPR and your student data

SPR (Student's Performance Report) is a free markbook made by Mohammed Shehzad Khan (Teacher Mo), an ESL teacher, to help teachers at his school record scores and print report cards. It is a teacher's tool. It is not a commercial product and it is not a school information system.

Whose data this is

The student records you enter belong to your school. The school decides who may use them and how long they are kept. The maker of SPR does not collect, hold, read or sell your data and has no way to open your classes.

Where data is kept

Data is stored inside the browser on the device you use. It is encrypted with your password. If you forget the password and no master password or backup exists, the data cannot be recovered by anyone. Exported Excel and backup files are not encrypted; keep them only on school-approved storage and delete them when no longer needed. Data is never stored unencrypted by SPR itself, whatever kind of connection you open it on.

Two teachers, one class

Classes can be shared between the two teachers of a class by exchanging an encrypted class file (Share → Download class file, then Import class from colleague). The file is ciphertext: without the class key it reveals nothing. When both teachers import, changes merge and any score cell changed by both is listed for a decision — nothing is overwritten silently. The class key travels only inside the share link, never to a server.

When data leaves the device

Nothing is sent anywhere unless an admin turns on Device sync or someone uses Save & Share. In that case an encrypted copy is stored on Supabase, a third-party hosting service. SPR does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Your part

Use a screen lock. Do not use shared or public computers. Download a backup after each working session — backups are the only protection against a browser clearing its storage. Keep backup files on school-approved storage and follow your school's rules and the law that applies to you (for example PIPL in China) when you handle or share student information. Written school approval should be recorded before real student data is entered.

No warranty

SPR is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may contain errors. Grades and comments it produces must be checked by a teacher before they are used. To the extent the law allows, the maker is not responsible for lost or corrupted data, wrong grades, or any use of the tool. Browsers can delete site data; backups are the only protection.
