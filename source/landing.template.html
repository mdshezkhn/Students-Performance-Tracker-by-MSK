A markbook for ESL classes.

Enter weekly scores, watch grades update, print report cards. Made by an ESL teacher for his own classes.

Open the app

What it does

  • Weekly score entry. Spelling, daily assignments, observations and tests, with auto-filled totals.
  • Automatic grades. A* to U, computed from the school’s formula (written 20%, every other part 10%).
  • Report cards. One page per student, printable, with comments you can hand-copy into the school performance book.
  • Works offline. After the first visit the app reopens without an internet connection.

Your data

  • Data lives in this browser, on this device. It is encrypted with your password.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless an admin turns on Device sync or someone uses Save & Share. Then an encrypted copy goes to Supabase.
  • Exported Excel and backup files are not encrypted. Keep them on school-approved storage.
  • The maker of SPR does not collect, read or sell your data.
