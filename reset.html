Start over on this device

This erases everything the SPR website has saved in this browser, on this computer — accounts, classes, scores and saved sign-ins — and returns the app to its very first screen.

  • Your other computers are not touched.
  • After this, you set up the school again in a couple of minutes: create the admin account, load your class, done.
  • Other teachers will re-connect to the new class with a fresh key (Manage & setup → Advanced → Key & link).
Tip: if you can still sign in on another computer where SPR works, you can first use Manage & setup → Advanced → Export everything there, and after this reset use Restore everything here to bring your class data back.
